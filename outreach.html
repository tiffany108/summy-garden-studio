Outreach desk

Owner account only.

Pull companies out of a page

Paste the address of any page that lists companies — a trade directory, a chamber of commerce member list, a conference exhibitor list, a "our clients" page. It reads the links and shows you the companies it found.

Or paste a list you already have

One company per line. A web address on its own is fine, or a name and address separated by a comma.

Look up their contact desk

Reads each company's own website for a published shared mailbox — hello@, info@, careers@ and the like — and notes how they describe themselves, so you have something to open with.

Only shared mailboxes are collected. A named individual's address is never taken, even when it is printed on the page. That is the difference between a list you can defend and one that generates a complaint.

Write the opening line

The tool gathers the evidence. You write the sentence — one true thing about them, in their words. A message with an empty line cannot be sent.

The message this campaign sends

Anything in double braces is filled in per company. {{company}}, {{domain}}, {{note}} (your line), {{title}}. Add a question mark — {{proof?}} — to make it optional, so an empty one simply disappears instead of blocking the send.

Every message gets a footer automatically — your name, your company, and a working way to say no. It is added after your template so it cannot be edited out by accident.
