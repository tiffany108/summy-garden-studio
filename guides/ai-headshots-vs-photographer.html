AI headshots vs a professional photographer

We sell AI headshots, so treat this with appropriate scepticism — but there are situations where hiring a photographer is plainly the better decision, and pretending otherwise helps nobody.

Choose a photographer when

  • You are the public face of a business. Founders, partners, executives and speakers should have real photographs.
  • You need consistency across a team page. One photographer, one lighting setup, one afternoon.
  • You need more than a headshot. Full-length, hands, props, environmental shots or anything with movement.
  • The photo has a long life. A press kit or book jacket justifies the investment.

Choose AI when

  • You need it this week. An application deadline does not wait for a studio booking.
  • The budget is not there. Especially for students and early-career applicants.
  • You want options. Several backgrounds and outfits to test what suits you.
  • You are updating a profile, not building a brand. Most people, most of the time.
The honest limitation: AI headshots reconstruct your face from the photos you provide. Give it sharp, well-lit images from several angles and the likeness is convincing. Give it one dark, distant photo and it will fill the gaps with something plausible but not quite you. The quality of the input sets the ceiling.

What to check before you accept any AI headshot

  1. Does it look like you at thumbnail size? That is how it will mostly be seen.
  2. Are the eyes right? Eye shape and spacing are what people unconsciously use to recognise a face.
  3. Is the skin texture still there? Over-smoothed skin is the clearest giveaway.
  4. Is anything odd at the edges? Ears, collars, glasses and hairlines are where errors hide.
  5. Would a colleague recognise you? The only test that really counts.

A reasonable middle path

Use an AI headshot now, because you need something decent today. Book a photographer when your circumstances justify it — a promotion, a company website, a public role. The two are not really in competition; they solve the same problem at very different price points and quality ceilings.

Common questions

Are AI headshots as good as a professional photographer?

For a LinkedIn profile or CV photo, they are close enough that most people cannot tell. A photographer still wins on true likeness, direction and posture, anything involving hands or full-body shots, and consistency across a team.

How much cheaper are AI headshots?

Typically $24-59 compared with $150-800 or more for a studio session, and ready in minutes rather than days or weeks.

When should I hire a real photographer instead?

When you are the public face of a business, when a whole team needs consistent photos, when you need full-length or environmental shots, or when the photograph will have a long life such as in a press kit.
